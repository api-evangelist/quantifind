EXPERTS ARE USING AI TO TRACE THE COMPLEX ORIGINS OF THE DRUGS IN AMERICAN’S MEDICINE CABINETS. PALO ALTO, Calif., November 30, 2022 – The U.S. Department of Homeland Security has approved research that will use artificial intelligence to delve into drug supply chains and probe for weaknesses, including foreign influence.
