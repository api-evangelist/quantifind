When a suspicious transaction surfaces inside a major bank, the race begins. Analysts open multiple systems, check sanctions lists, search the news for adverse media, and scan public records, all before any real judgment can even begin. It is a process that has remained largely unchanged in a decade, despite growing technology budgets.
